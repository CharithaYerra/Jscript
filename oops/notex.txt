1. Encapsulation
Definition: Wrapping data (variables) and methods (functions) together into a single unit (class).

Goal: Hide the internal state of the object and only expose what is necessary.

2. Abstraction
Definition: Hiding complex internal details and showing only essential features.

Goal: Reduce complexity and isolate the impact of changes.


3. Inheritance
Definition: One class (child) inherits the properties and behaviors of another class (parent).

Goal: Code reusability.

4. Polymorphism
Definition: One thing can take many forms.

Types:

Compile-time polymorphism (Method Overloading)

Run-time polymorphism (Method Overriding)
